The liquid is drawn by the same code as on the Vita, carried over line for line, so the motion, colours and shading are what the console shows. The text is in the app's own two fonts, Poppins and Righteous for the name, though the Vita draws the letters with its own font library, so their spacing can differ a little. Nothing is installed from this page, and it doesn't go online: choosing Install, Uninstall or Check for a new version only shows what the app would report.
